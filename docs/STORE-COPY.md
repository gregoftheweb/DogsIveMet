## Description [4000]

Dogs I've Met is a simple way to remember the dogs in your life: the golden retriever on your morning walk, the neighbour's new puppy, the poodle at the dog park whose name you can never recall.

Add a dog in seconds. Take a photo, then say its name, its owner and where you met instead of typing: tap the microphone next to any field and talk. Pick the breed from the list, add a note if you like, and save.

Find them again later. Browse every dog you've met, newest first, filter by breed, or search by name by voice or keyboard. Each dog has its own page with the photo, owner, breed, the date you met and where.

Keep your own dogs too. My Dogs holds your own pets alongside everyone else's.

Make it yours. Light or dark mode, and a choice of highlight colours.

Private by design. There's no account and no sign-in. Your dogs, photos and notes stay on your phone and are never uploaded. The app is free and shows a small banner ad.

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Dogs I've Met is a personal log of the dogs a person meets: dog walkers, dog park regulars and neighbours who want to remember dogs' names, breeds and owners. The value is a quick way to save a dog with a photo and spoken details, and to find it again later by name or breed. Everything is stored only on the device.

3. HOW TO USE THE APP
No account, login, setup or sample files are needed.
- Launch the app. Tap New Dog.
- Tap Take Photo to photograph a dog (or skip it). Tap the microphone button beside Name, Owner or Where did I meet this dog? and speak; tap the keyboard icon to type instead. Choose a breed, then tap Save Dog.
- All Dogs lists every dog, newest first, with a breed filter and search by name (the microphone works there too). Tap a dog to see its profile.
- My Dogs keeps the user's own dogs. Settings has light/dark mode, highlight colours and the privacy policy.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
All of the app's data (dogs, photos, notes) stays on the device; there is no server, account or analytics. Voice dictation uses Apple's speech recognition (SFSpeechRecognizer) only while the user is dictating. The one outside service is Google AdMob, which shows a banner ad at the bottom of the screen. Before ads start, the app shows Google's consent form where the law requires it and Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; if the user declines tracking, ads are still shown but are not personalised.
